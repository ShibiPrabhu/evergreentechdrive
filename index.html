About us

A trusted name in industrial transmission

Evergreen TechDrive is a dependable supplier of industrial transmission products, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. We concentrate on gear motors, gearboxes and drive systems, offering reliable solutions built for the demanding needs of manufacturing and industrial operations.

Our focus on quality, competitive pricing and expert technical support has made us a preferred partner for businesses across Tamil Nadu and beyond.

What we supply

Nord Gear Motors

A leading wholesale supplier of Nord Unicase Inline Helical Geared Motors, Nord Unicase Parallel Shaft Gear Motors, the Nord B5010 DuoDrive Geared Motor, the Nord Max Drive XC Right Angle Gear Motor, the Nord Max Drive XC Parallel Shaft Gear Motor and the Nord Unicase Worm Gear Motor, all from Chennai.

Electric Motor

Nord's new, energy-efficient universal motor widens the company's motor range for key world markets, including Europe, the USA, Canada and China.

Asynchronous Electric Motor

This energy-efficient universal motor from Nord drive systems expands the portfolio for major markets worldwide, such as Europe, the USA, Canada and China.

Synchronous Motors

Nord's latest generation of IE5+ synchronous motors is built in-house and designed for intralogistics as well as the food and beverage industry.

Explosion Proof Motors

Nord's gas explosion-protected motors are manufactured at its own facilities and have been delivered to all major markets around the world since 2003.

Variable Frequency Drive & Inverter

The Nordac pro is a variable frequency drive and inverter for every kind of drive application. Even the basic SK 500P version delivers excellent versatility.
