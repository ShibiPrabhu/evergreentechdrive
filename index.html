About us

A trusted name in industrial transmission.

Evergreen TechDrive is a dependable supplier of industrial transmission products, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

We concentrate on gear motors, gearboxes and drive systems, offering reliable solutions built for the demanding needs of manufacturing and industrial operations.

Our focus on quality, competitive pricing and expert technical support has made us a preferred partner for businesses across Tamil Nadu and beyond.

Authorised supply

Our brand: Nord.

Everything in our range comes from Nord Drivesystems, from geared motors and electric motors to frequency inverters.

Nord Gear Motors
